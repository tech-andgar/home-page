About

Hi, I’m Andrés García, a Deaf Software Engineer specializing in mobile and web applications. Colombian Sign Language (LSC) is my native language, and I work primarily with Flutter and Dart for mobile development.

Currently, I’m a Senior Software Engineer at Slalom Consulting in Bogota. I also collaborate with accessibility-focused organizations like Sign Language Tech on community projects.
