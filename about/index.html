About

Hi, I’m Andrés García, a Deaf Software Engineer with 7+ years of experience building mobile and web applications. Colombian Sign Language (LSC) is my native language, and I work primarily with Flutter and Dart for mobile development.

Quick highlights:

  • Helped build Flutter apps used by millions of users at Bancolombia (Colombia’s largest bank)
  • Reduced technical debt by 40% and CI/CD pipeline time by 50% in production apps
  • Direct communication in sign language = no barriers, no misunderstandings with Deaf teams/clients
  • Active open-source contributor with packages used by thousands of developers

Currently, I’m a Senior Software Engineer at Slalom Consulting in Bogota. I also collaborate with accessibility-focused organizations like Sign Language Tech on community projects.

Work preference: Remote | Full-time

Languages: Spanish (B2) | English (B2) | Colombian Sign Language (native). For spoken meetings, I use sign language interpretation and live captions (Zoom, Teams, Meet).

What people say Link to heading

Clients and teammates have mentioned that I’m good at understanding what they actually need. One client (Eduard Dobra from JOF Foundation) said working with me was “seamless” because we could communicate directly in sign language without anything getting lost. That means a lot to me - being able to connect directly with people, whether they’re Deaf or hearing, is something I care about.

My colleagues have noted that I’m proactive about learning new things and not afraid to step outside my comfort zone. I try to be helpful to the team, share what I know, and keep improving.

How I work Link to heading

I try to follow architectural patterns like Clean Architecture and BLoC/Riverpod for state management in Flutter. I apply SOLID principles, use Atomic Design for UI components, and believe in testing my code. These aren’t just buzzwords for me - they help me write code that’s easier to maintain and test.

My background Link to heading

I studied at SENA (National Training Service of Colombia) where I got my Technological Specialization in Mobile App Development and Technology degree in Information Systems Analysis. Before getting into software development, I actually started in IT support and hardware maintenance - that’s where I learned how technology really works from the ground up.

I’ve worked at companies like Capgemini (where I spent almost 3 years building Flutter apps for Bancolombia) and Cafeto Software (developing healthcare apps for LogicMark). Each job taught me something new about working in teams, handling real production code, and dealing with the messy parts of software that textbooks don’t cover.

Notable certifications: Microsoft MTA Database Administration (98-364)
